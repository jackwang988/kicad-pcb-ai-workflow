# LED matrix independent design basis, revision 1

User requirements: 60x60 mm, 8x8/64 2835 LEDs, 7.5 mm pitch, 3.75 mm LED-center edge inset. All LEDs face front and share upper anode/lower cathode orientation. 16 parallel branches, each four series LEDs and one 1206 resistor. Front-only copper and components, no vias/jumpers. Four edge-midpoint +/- solder-pad pairs, same polarity interconnected.

Selected test LED: Wurth Elektronik 150283RS73103, red 2835. Manufacturer schematic pin 1=A, pin 2=K. Recommended pads: A 2.1x2.1 mm, K 1.0x2.1 mm, gap 0.4 mm, total X span 3.5 mm. The large anode land is at native x=-0.70, cathode at x=+1.25. Source: official PDF https://www.we-online.com/components/products/datasheet/150283RS73103.pdf (revision 001.003, 2026-07-06 viewed through web). Raw older manufacturer-authored revision 001.001 mirrored at https://se.ariat-tech.com/datasheet/81/150283RS73103.pdf, pages 1-3, is retained explicitly as a mirror, not an official download. Both reviewed dimensions agree. A project symbol uses actual manufacturer numbering 1=A/2=K.

Engineering choices for this low-current workflow test: 1k ohm 1% 1206 0.25 W per branch, regulated bench supply nominal 12 V, design calculation range 11.4-12.6 V. At approximate 2 V per LED, branch current is about 4 mA and input about 64 mA/0.77 W. The 150 mA datasheet Vf bin is NOT a guarantee at this lower current; brightness/current require prototype measurement. Even the conservative zero-LED-drop arithmetic bound is 12.6/990=12.73 mA and resistor 12.6^2/990=0.161 W, below the 0.25 W rating at room temperature. No white-LED substitution is approved without rechecking headroom.

Physical design intent: single copper FR-4, nominal thickness 1.6 mm, nominal 35 um copper, front-only routing; these are design assumptions, not a supplier acceptance. Minimum electrical clearance 0.25 mm, branch track 0.30 mm, supply trunks 0.60 mm or wider where possible, copper-to-finished-edge >=0.25 mm. No mounting holes. Native KiCad may retain an empty logical B.Cu; manufacturing exports must contain only F.Cu and explicitly state SINGLE COPPER SIDE. B.Cu must have zero pads/tracks/zones/vias.

Native A3 landscape worksheet: default title/metadata area conservatively reserved x230..415/y247..292 mm; component functional area is x20..395/y15..220 mm. Bottom-left x20..210/y230..265 reserved for power pads/notes. Actual exported view remains mandatory.

Independent branch graph: column c=0..7, half h=0..1, branch b=2*c+h+1. D refs are D(8*c+4*h+1)..D(8*c+4*h+4), R is Rb. Even columns: +12V -> D1 -> D2 -> R -> D3 -> D4 -> GND. Odd columns: +12V -> D1 -> D2 -> D3 -> R -> D4 -> GND. All LED traversal is A pin1 to K pin2. Resistor traversal is pin1 to pin2. J1/J2/J3/J4 pin1=+12V, pin2=GND.
