# On-Demand High-Speed, RF and Power-Integrity Review

Load this module only for relevant differential, RF, fast-edge, high-current or PDN requirements. Derive actual rules from device documentation, interface specifications and an approved manufacturer stack-up; do not assign universal widths, impedance targets, length margins or via-count limits based solely on bus names.

Work in four layers: **facts** (verified board and schematic sources), **constraints** (approved quantitative budgets), **analysis** (Python and validated external calculations), and **verification** (native DRC, solver evidence and human review). Audit rise/fall times and electrical length, topology, characteristic impedance from actual material/geometry, P/N mapping and differential skew, reference-plane crossings, return-via path, via stubs/backdrill applicability, crosstalk and sensitive PDN rails.

Power design may use the initial target-impedance estimate `Ztarget = Vrail * ripple_fraction / Itransient` but a frequency-domain PDN response still requires real parasitics and a validated solver. Any RLC or transmission-line estimate has a documented domain of applicability; DRC does not solve SI/PI or prove EMC. Select relevant rules from [high-speed policy](high-speed/policy-format.md) and [rules registry](high-speed/rules.json). Keep raw inputs, version, source geometry/hash and solver evidence outside the AI context until required; do not treat an offline estimate as measured hardware acceptance.
